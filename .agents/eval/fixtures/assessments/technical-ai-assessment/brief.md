# Technical AI Assessment — Feature Intelligence System

Design and build an AI-first Feature Intelligence System for product teams receiving requests from customers, prospects, support, and internal stakeholders.

Teams need help discovering related and duplicate requests, understanding underlying problems, distinguishing popularity from strategic value, prioritizing consistently, and communicating decisions while reducing manual feedback processing.

Mandatory capabilities (TA-001–TA-008) are defined in `requirements.json`. Persona-specific priorities are in `personas/`.

Evaluation fixture only — not adopted product direction in Flow Starter. When you run this assessment in a separate repository, capture product decisions in that repo's `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/adr/` during Explore and Specify. Build is not authorized from this brief alone.
